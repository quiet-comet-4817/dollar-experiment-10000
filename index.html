One simple question about generosity, curiosity, and what can happen when a small idea reaches a lot of people.

The goal

The target is 10,000 voluntary $1 contributions. Progress is updated manually; it is not connected to PayPal automatically.

Full transparency

This is not charity. Any money voluntarily sent goes to the project creator, who keeps it. There is no prize, repayment, financial return, or promised benefit.
  • Taking part is completely optional.
  • You do not need to send money to follow the experiment.
  • The progress numbers are manually reported and may not update instantly.
  • Please only participate if you understand the terms and are comfortable with them.
